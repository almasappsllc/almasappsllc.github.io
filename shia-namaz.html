Shia-Specific Mathematics

Maghrib ≠ Sunset

Calculated precisely as 4° (Qom) or 4.5° (Tehran) below the horizon, usually 13–17 minutes after sunset.

Asr Calculation

Uses shadow factor 1 according to the Ja'fari school of thought.

Shari'i Midnight

Calculated accurately as the midpoint between Maghrib and the following day's Fajr.
