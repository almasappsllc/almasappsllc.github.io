Privacy Policy — Pulse

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Pulse (“the app”) is developed by Almas Apps LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Health and Fitness Data

The app connects to external Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) heart rate monitors to display your real-time heart rate data, and can record completed workouts (duration, time in heart-rate zones, average/min/max heart rate, an activity type and an optional note). All of it is processed and stored entirely locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share your personal health data with any external servers. There is no account and no Pulse cloud.

Camera

Pulse can estimate your heart rate from a fingertip held over the rear camera and flash. The camera is used as a sensor: frames are analysed in memory on your device to detect the pulse signal and are never saved, recorded, or uploaded. This feature is optional and only runs while its screen is open.

Data you choose to send elsewhere

Nothing leaves your device unless you ask it to. Two features can, and both are off until you use them:

  • Health Connect — if you turn it on and grant permission, completed workouts and heart-rate samples are written to Android's Health Connect on your device. Pulse only writes; it never reads your Health Connect data. What happens to it after that is governed by Health Connect and any other app you have granted access to.
  • CSV export — you can export your own session history to a file you choose, through the system's save dialog. The file is written wherever you point it, and Pulse has no copy of it.

You can delete your entire on-device history at any time from Settings, and uninstalling the app removes it.

Purchases

Pulse Pro is an optional subscription sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google — Pulse never sees your card details, and there is no account to create. We receive only whether the subscription is currently active, in order to unlock the features it includes.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect certain information about your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, and device analytics, to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. For more information on how Google collects and uses your data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions we request

  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_SCAN / BLUETOOTH_CONNECT) — to scan for and connect to external heart rate monitors.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) — required by some Android versions to allow Bluetooth scanning. Your location is never tracked or stored.
  • Internet (INTERNET) — to load and display advertisements via Google AdMob, and to complete purchases through Google Play.
  • Camera (CAMERA) — for the optional fingertip heart-rate reading. Frames are never saved or uploaded.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — for the ongoing workout notification, zone alerts, and low-battery or disconnect warnings.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE) — for optional alerts when you drift out of your target zone.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE) — to keep the connection to your heart-rate monitor and the workout clock alive while the screen is off.
  • Health Connect (health.WRITE_EXERCISE, health.WRITE_HEART_RATE) — only if you enable Health Connect export. Write-only; Pulse never reads your health records.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences but requires users to be 18 or older to comply with advertising network policies. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.
