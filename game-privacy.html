The security of your data is important to us. All data collected by third-party services is encrypted in transit. Please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Our App is intended for a general audience and is targeted to users who are 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 18.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
