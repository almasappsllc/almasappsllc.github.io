How to

Using the Qibla calculator

Get a bearing with a city, your current location or coordinates you already know.

Choose a city

Select one of the listed cities to calculate a bearing from its approximate center. This is useful for a quick estimate, but a city can cover a wide area. Use your own coordinates for a more specific result.

Use your current location

Select “Use my current location” and allow the browser to share it. The calculator uses the coordinates in your browser to work out a bearing. The site does not send those coordinates to a server. You can decline permission and still use the other options.

Enter coordinates

Open the manual entry section and enter latitude and longitude in decimal degrees. Latitude ranges from −90° to 90°; longitude ranges from −180° to 180°. Keep south and west coordinates negative.

Read the result

The result is degrees clockwise from true north. The arrow points toward the bearing on the diagram. It is a map direction, not the current heading of your phone.

Need help finding coordinates?
Most map apps show latitude and longitude when you press and hold a location pin.
