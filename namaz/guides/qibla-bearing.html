Qibla

What does a Qibla bearing tell you?

A bearing turns a direction into a number you can use on a map or with a compass.

A Qibla bearing is the direction from your location toward the Kaaba in Makkah. It is usually written as an angle in degrees, measured clockwise from true north.

How to read the number

Imagine standing at your location and facing true north. Turn clockwise by the number of degrees shown. A bearing of 90° points due east; 180° points south; 270° points west. Most locations fall somewhere between those cardinal directions.

The calculator on this site finds the initial bearing along the shortest great-circle route between your coordinates and the Kaaba. Because Earth is curved, that direction can differ from a straight line drawn on a flat map, especially over long distances.

True north and magnetic north

A map’s north is true north. A handheld magnetic compass points toward magnetic north, which varies by location. A phone compass also depends on its sensors and can be affected by nearby metal or electronics. This calculator gives a map bearing; it does not read or correct your device compass.
