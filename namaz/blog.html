Short answers about Ja'fari prayer times.

How many rak'ahs is each Shia prayer?

4 November 2026

The five daily prayers have 17 rak'ahs: Fajr 2, Dhuhr 4, Asr 4, Maghrib 3 and Isha 4.

How do I find the Qibla without the internet?

4 November 2026

You can find the Qibla with no internet. Use your phone's compass, or the sun on two days a year.

Why is Shia Maghrib after sunset?

3 November 2026

Most prayer apps put Maghrib at sunset. Ja'fari practice waits until the eastern redness has passed overhead, usually 15 to 30 minutes later.
