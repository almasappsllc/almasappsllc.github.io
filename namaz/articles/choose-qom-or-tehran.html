GUIDE 03 · APP SETTINGS

Qom or Tehran: which method should I choose?

Both are Ja'fari presets in Namaz. They differ in the solar angles used for dawn and Maghrib.

About 3 minutes · Values taken from the Shia Namaz app's engine

The settings in Namaz

The app offers two named presets. Each specifies a sun angle for Fajr, Isha and Maghrib. The sun's position, your coordinates and date turn those settings into local times.

PresetFajrIshaMaghrib
Qom (default)16.0°14.0°4.0°
Tehran17.7°14.0°4.5°

In both presets, Asr uses a shadow factor of one. Namaz calculates the solar events with the Adhan Kotlin library, then applies its own Ja'fari-specific Maghrib and midnight handling.

How to choose

Start with the method your mosque, marja' or trusted local timetable recommends. If it uses one of these presets, select that one. If it gives different angles, Namaz's Custom option lets you enter them.

These are not universal labels for every Ja'fari calculation in use. The right setting depends on the authority and convention you follow. If you're unsure, ask your mosque which method it uses rather than choosing based on a city name alone.

Changing the method can move Fajr and Maghrib. Compare the resulting timetable with a trusted local source before relying on it.
