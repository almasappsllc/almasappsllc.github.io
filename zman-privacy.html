Privacy Policy — Zman

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Zman (“the app”) for iPhone is developed by Almas Apps (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. In short: the app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It is designed to work entirely offline.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It contains no third-party SDKs of any kind, and it makes no network requests of its own.

Location

The app uses your iPhone’s location only on your iPhone to calculate sunrise, sunset, candle lighting and other times, and the direction of Jerusalem. Your location is never collected, stored on our servers, or transmitted anywhere. You can choose a city from the app’s built-in offline list instead, and leave location access off entirely. The place the app last used is kept on your iPhone, in the app’s own storage, so the times appear at once the next time you open it.

Reminders and widgets

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your iPhone’s own notification system; nothing is sent through a server. Widgets read the same settings as the app, on your iPhone, through an app group shared only between Zman and its own widget.

Purchases

The app offers an optional Pro upgrade (widgets and extra color themes) as a yearly subscription or a one-time purchase, processed entirely by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your Apple Account settings. We never see or store your card number, name or payment details; we receive only anonymized sales reports from Apple. Times, the calendar, the Jerusalem compass and reminders are free.

Permissions the app requests

  • Location (while using the app) — to compute times and the direction of Jerusalem on your iPhone. Optional; choosing a city works without it.
  • Notifications — only if you turn reminders on.
  • Motion and compass — read by the system while the compass screen is open, to point at Jerusalem.

Third-party data

City names come from the GeoNames geographical database (CC BY 4.0), bundled inside the app. Nothing is downloaded.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.
