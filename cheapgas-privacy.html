Privacy Policy — CheapGas

Last updated: August 19, 2026

CheapGas (“the app”) is developed by Almas Apps LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Location and Gas Data

The app uses your device’s location to find nearby gas stations and their prices. Your location data is processed to retrieve this information, but we do not collect, store, or share your personal identity or persistent location history with any external servers.

Advertising

The app may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect certain information about your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, and device analytics, to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. For more information on how Google collects and uses your data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions we request

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) — to find nearby gas stations and display their prices based on your current location.
  • Internet (INTERNET) — to fetch the latest gas prices and load advertisements via Google AdMob.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences but requires users to be 18 or older to comply with advertising network policies. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
