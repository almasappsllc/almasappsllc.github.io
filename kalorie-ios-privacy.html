Privacy Policy — Kalorie for iPhone

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Kalorie (“the app”) is developed by Almas Apps LLC (“we”, “us”). This page covers the iPhone app. In short: the app collects no personal data. It has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your food diary

Everything you enter (logged meals, targets, body measurements, weigh-ins, water, notes, fasts, custom foods, recipes and favourites) is stored only on your iPhone. We operate no server and never see it. The food database ships inside the app, so searching or scanning a food never leaves your phone. Your iPhone's own backup (iCloud or a computer) may include the app's data under Apple's policies, as it does for every app.

Google Sheet sync (optional)

If you choose to, the app can send each day's totals to a Google Sheet you own, through a Google Apps Script web app you deploy yourself. It is off until you paste your script's address in Settings. The data goes directly from your iPhone to your own script on Google's servers; we have no access to your Google account, your sheet or the data sent. This is the only network request the app makes. Google's handling of it is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Camera

Used only when you open the barcode scanner. Frames are decoded on your iPhone and are never stored or sent. Refuse it and everything works except the scanner.

Notifications

Requested only if you turn on the alert that tells you a fast has reached its target. It is scheduled by your iPhone; nothing passes through a server.

Purchases

Kalorie Pro is offered as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, processed entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never see your name or payment details; we receive only anonymised sales reports. Subscriptions renew until cancelled in your Apple Account settings.

Backups and exports

Backup files and CSV exports are created on your iPhone (Files › On My iPhone › Kalorie) and leave it only when you share or move them yourself.

Crash reports

The app has no crash-reporting SDK. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymised crash reports under Apple's policies.

Food data

The food database is derived from Health Canada's Canadian Nutrient File, USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts contributors (ODbL). Nutrition figures are estimates, not medical advice.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if advertising were added), this page will be updated in or before the release that introduces it.
