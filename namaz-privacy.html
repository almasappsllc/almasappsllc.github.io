Privacy Policy — Shia Namaz

Last updated: 24 June 2026

Shia Namaz (“the app”) is developed by Almas Apps (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. In short: the app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It is designed to work entirely offline.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not contain any third-party tracking SDKs.

Location

The app uses your device’s location only on your device to calculate accurate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Your location is never collected, stored on our servers, or transmitted anywhere — the app does not request the Internet permission and cannot send data off your device. You may also enter a location manually and leave GPS off entirely.

Tips and purchases

The app offers an optional “tip” to support development. These payments are processed entirely by Google Play under Google’s Privacy Policy. We never see or store your card number or payment details; we only receive anonymized payout information from Google. Tips are optional and unlock no features — the app is fully free.

Permissions we request

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) — to compute prayer times and Qibla on your device. Optional; manual entry is supported.
  • Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) — added by Google Play Billing so the optional tip can be processed by the Play Store.

The app does not request Internet access.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
