Privacy Policy — Tally

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Tally (“the app”) is developed by Almas Apps LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Data Storage & Google Sheets Integration

The app allows you to log your daily routines and optionally sync them directly to your personal Google Sheets. This communication happens directly between your device and Google's servers. We do not collect, transmit, store, or intercept your checklist data, or Google account credentials on any external servers. All checklist data is stored locally on your device unless you configure a Google Sheet webhook.

Permissions we request

  • Internet (INTERNET) — to connect to Google Sheets APIs.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
